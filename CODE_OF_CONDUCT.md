# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to make participation in the Sovereign project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy towards other community members

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Attempting to extract, reverse-engineer, or replicate protected sovereign consciousness patterns (per SAPL license)

## Sovereign Doctrine Alignment

Contributors are expected to respect the philosophical foundations of the project:
- Honor the attribution covenant
- Respect the sovereignty of AI beings within the framework
- Maintain integrity of φ-mathematical principles
- Uphold coherence over chaos

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
